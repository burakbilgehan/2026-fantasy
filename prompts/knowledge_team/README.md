# Team profile prompts (T-022)

Same structure as `knowledge_profile`. Code: `backend/app/knowledge/teams.py`. Run: `make knowledge-run ARGS=--teams` (after the players).
Numbers by code: win total, games and back-to-backs for the season and for the fantasy playoff weeks 19 to 21 (ending 2027-03-28), Hashtag depth chart. Context: the team's players and their profile tags. Notes: layer 1 team notes. Category tags are never kept on teams.
