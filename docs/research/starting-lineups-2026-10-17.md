# Opening-night starting lineups, 2026-27

Research of 2026-10-17 (season opens 2026-10-20). Five web research agents, one per six teams, read news from 2026-10-10 to 2026-10-17 (coach quotes, preseason starters, beat writers). The main session checked the Day'Ron Sharpe conflict in Chrome (ESPN injuries and depth chart, 2026-10-17).

Status: `confirmed` = coach or team said so, or he started the preseason with the main group and beat writers agree. `very likely` = consistent reports, no real rival. `disputed` = two or more players compete. `role up` = last season mostly a bench or small-role player (under about half the games started, or under about 25 minutes), now a confirmed or very likely starter. Rookies are never `role up`.

Web news is newer than our expert notes in `docs/knowledge/profiles/`; where they conflict, the agent says so in the row.
