## Setup
- Base: ESPN 2025-26 preseason projection, 381 players. Truth: stats.nba.com 2025-26 totals.
- 17 projected players have no 2025-26 games. 2 of the league's 144 drafted players have no ESPN projection; models cannot buy them.
- Weeks: Monday to Sunday, 2025-10-20 to 2026-03-23, 23 weeks (assumed; Yahoo week 1 and All-Star week can differ).
- H2H test: a 13th team per model buys exactly 12 players with 200 USD. Price = the real 2025-26 price in our league; undrafted players cost 1 USD. The team maximizes the model's preseason dollars. It plays every week against each of the 12 real draft rosters.
- Draft and hold for all 13 teams: no waivers, no trades, no lineup cap, positions ignored, injured players score 0. Ties count 0.5.
- Cat %: share of category matchups won. Match %: share of weekly matchups won. Place: where the cat % would rank among the 12 real teams (their cat % is all-play against the other 11).
- One roster per model is mostly injury luck. So every model buys 30 rosters, each with bidding noise (assumed): price x U(0.8, 1.25), undrafted players 1 to 3 USD. Cat % and match % are means over the draws. SD: spread of the cat % over the draws. One season only.
